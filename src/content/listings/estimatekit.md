## What it is

EstimateKit is browser-based home-service software for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical shops. You build branded estimates from a trade price book, offer good/better/best options, send a link by email or text from your own phone, and the customer picks an option and signs in the browser. Approved quotes become jobs on a drag-and-drop calendar, then invoices. Connect Stripe and customers can pay by card from the invoice link into your own Stripe account; EstimateKit says it adds no payment fee of its own. Customers, invoices, and payments can sync one way into QuickBooks Online.

Each trade starts with a priced book: the HVAC page says a new HVAC account opens with **41 repairs** in seven groups plus six good/better/best quotes (2-, 3-, and 4-ton AC replacements, a furnace, a heat pump, and a tune-up). Plumbing and electrical pages each advertise **45 repairs** with starter templates (water heaters, panels, EV chargers, and similar). You set your hourly rate and markup once and the book re-prices. Maintenance plans, equipment records (model, serial, install date, data-plate photo), CSV customer import, and offline estimate editing are included.

The vendor is explicit about what it is not: **not a plan takeoff tool**. Commercial and new-construction bids measured from drawings belong in tools such as [PlanSwift](../planswift/), [STACK](../stack/), or trade-specific bid software. EstimateKit is for pricing known service and replacement jobs at the house.

## Who it's for

EstimateKit targets small and medium residential home-service shops in the US and Canada, usually from one truck up to 25 people, where the owner still writes quotes on paper, in Excel, or in a basic invoicing app. The about page names HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and similar trades doing replacements, repairs, and maintenance.

It is a poor fit for shops that need two-way SMS, call tracking, live GPS truck tracking, tap-to-pay, customer financing, QuickBooks Desktop, or more than 25 users, and for anyone measuring ductwork or devices off drawings.

For a simpler multi-trade estimate-and-invoice app without trade price books, compare [Joist](../joist/). Electricians and plumbers who bid from plans with labor units should look at [TurboBid](../turbobid/) or, for electrical-only commercial bidding, [Electrical Bid Manager](../electrical-bid-manager/) instead.

We found almost no independent contractor reviews. A search of Reddit and X turned up promotional posts and directory listings rather than shop owners describing day-to-day use.

## Pricing (verified November 5, 2026)

| Plan | Price (per vendor) | Included | Notable limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EstimateKit (one plan) | $49/mo, billed monthly | Unlimited estimates and invoices, scheduling, Stripe payments, QuickBooks Online sync, up to 25 users, every listed feature | No annual plan published; 25-user cap |

There are no tiers, no per-seat fees, and no setup fee. The vendor says cancel anytime from account settings with no cancellation fee; partial months are not refunded. After the **14-day free trial** (no card), you are asked to subscribe; if you do nothing, the account becomes read-only and data stays until you return or ask for deletion.

**Currency:** Pages show bare "$49" without a USD or CAD code. The company is in Abbotsford, BC, Canada, and markets to the US and Canada. Terms say prices are "in the currency shown at checkout." The pricing FAQ gives Stripe's Canadian card example as **2.9% + CA$0.30**. Confirm the currency on the Stripe checkout screen before you subscribe; we could not independently verify whether a US shop is charged USD or CAD.

Competitor comparison tables on EstimateKit's site (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan) are vendor-published and illustrative; we did not re-verify those third-party prices for this review.

## Platforms

Runs in a web browser on phone and desktop. The vendor says it installs to the home screen as a PWA and that estimates work with no cell signal. Native App Store / Google Play apps are "built and in review" but were not available when we checked. There is no Windows desktop installer.

## What we couldn't verify

- Whether the $49 subscription is billed in USD, CAD, or either depending on account location (currency is shown at Stripe checkout).
- Real-world accuracy of the starter HVAC, plumbing, and electrical price books against local labor and supplier costs.
- Independent contractor reviews; public mentions we found were largely promotional.
- Exact Stripe fees outside Canada, and when "US automatic sales tax" will ship.
- Whether native mobile apps have shipped since this review (vendor said they were in review).
