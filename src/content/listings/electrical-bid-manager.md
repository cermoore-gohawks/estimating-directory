## What it is

Electrical Bid Manager (EBM) is Windows desktop estimating software for electrical contractors from Vision InfoSoft, a company that has sold electrical and plumbing estimating software since 1993 and is now part of the JDM Technology Group. You build a bid with takeoff modules for branch circuits, lighting, wiring devices, and other electrical scopes, using a database of items and pre-built assemblies with labor units. Bid analysis then lets you add non-productive labor, subcontracts, overhead, and profit, and produce a proposal.

Vision also sells the EPIC material pricing service (trade and target prices for about 2 million items, with optional daily commodity updates through an add-on called DUO), a Time & Material Billing Manager for service billing, and a Plumbing Bid Manager.

EBM doesn't have its own PDF measuring engine. For digital plans it links with [PlanSwift](../planswift/) or On-Screen Takeoff, which Vision resells along with the integration link. For a lower-cost electrical and plumbing option aimed at residential and light commercial work, compare [TurboBid](../turbobid/). If you mostly quote service calls on site rather than bid from plans, [EstimateKit](../estimatekit/) is a closer fit.

## Who it's for

Electrical contractors and estimators bidding residential, commercial, and industrial jobs from plans, from one-person shops to companies with several estimators sharing a database. The Pro edition adds specialty databases for pole line, high voltage, explosion proof, and fire alarm work.

A poor fit for Mac users, multi-trade general contractors, and service electricians who need quoting, scheduling, and invoicing in one app.

## Pricing (verified November 9, 2026)

From Vision InfoSoft's version comparison page, in USD:

| Edition | Listed price | What the comparison table adds (per vendor) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $1,549 | 13 takeoff modules, 1 specialty database (VDV and demolition), bid analysis, change orders, supplier quotes |
| Plus | $2,749 | 3 specialty databases, adds the residential specialty database |
| Pro | $4,549 | 17 takeoff modules, 7 specialty databases including pole line, high voltage, explosion proof, and fire alarm |
| Audit Trail editions | Call | Advanced substitution, undo, filtering, copying between jobs |

- The comparison page doesn't say whether these prices are one-time or recurring. The main product page says you can "get started for less than $1,000/year" and offers interest-free payment plans. Some third-party sites describe the prices as perpetual licenses that include the first year of subscription; we couldn't confirm that on Vision's own pages.
- The annual Software Subscription covers version and database updates, toll-free phone support with a 2-hour turnaround guarantee, and 25% off add-ons, extra users, and training. Its price isn't published.
- EPIC pricing, the DUO and Catlynx add-ons, the PlanSwift or On-Screen Takeoff integration link, and hands-on training are sold separately.

## Platforms

Windows 8, 10, or 11 on Intel or AMD processors, with 4 GB RAM minimum (8 GB recommended). The vendor says it isn't compatible with Mac or ARM-based Windows machines, and recommends dual monitors for plan takeoff. Vision says you can run it on Windows cloud virtual machines from providers such as Microsoft or V2Cloud.

## What we couldn't verify

- Whether the $1,549 to $4,549 prices are one-time or annual, and what the yearly subscription and EPIC pricing cost.
- The price of the PlanSwift or On-Screen Takeoff integration link.
- Database size: the product page says over 10,000 items and assemblies, while the comparison page says over 20,000 standard items.
- Vendor claims such as cutting estimating time "in half."
