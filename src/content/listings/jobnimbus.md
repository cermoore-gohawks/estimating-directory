## What it is

JobNimbus is a CRM and project-management platform for roofing and exterior contractors. It acquired SumoQuote, a roofing proposal tool (forum posts from early 2024 discuss the deal), and the vendor says the SumoQuote estimating experience is now built directly into JobNimbus. If you're searching for SumoQuote today, this is where it lives.

On the estimating side, the vendor lists branded homeowner proposals, good/better/best pricing options, supplier-integrated material pricing, digital signatures, and monthly-payment financing offered inside the proposal. You can build and sign estimates in the mobile app on site. Around that, JobNimbus covers leads and appointments, customizable job boards, automations, crew scheduling, production tracking, invoices, JobNimbus Payments, and reporting.

It doesn't measure roofs itself. Pair it with a measurement report or tool; if you want measurements and quoting in one lighter app, compare [Roofr](../roofr/) or [RoofSnap](../roofsnap/).

## Who it's for

The vendor says it's built for home exterior contractors, "specifically ones who work primarily in roofing, restoration roofing, solar, gutters, and siding," from single-crew shops to multi-location sales teams. Reviewers and forum posters most often recommend it to insurance-restoration roofers with several salespeople. Small retail-only crews often find it more than they need.

## Pricing (checked November 13, 2026)

JobNimbus doesn't publish dollar prices. Its pricing page lists these plans:

| Plan | Users | Project boards | Automations | Integrations | Multi-location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essentials | Up to 3 | 3 | 10 | 2 | No |
| Pro | Up to 10 | 5 | 30 | 5 | Up to 2 |
| Premium | Up to 19 | Unlimited | 100 | Unlimited | Up to 5 |
| Enterprise | 20+ | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

- Every plan includes unlimited contacts, estimates, documents, eSign, reports, invoices, financing options, supplier integrations, QuickBooks integration, and JobNimbus Payments, per the vendor.
- Price depends on plan, user count, and add-ons. The FAQ describes an account-based CRM price plus role-based pricing for admin, sales, and field users.
- Optional Marketing Bundle and Communications Bundle (Engage texting) cost extra.
- The vendor says there are no required setup fees, offers monthly or annual agreements, and has a 14-day free trial with no credit card.

## Platforms

Web browser, plus native iOS and Android apps. When checked, the App Store listing showed 4.8 from about 13,000 ratings, and Google Play showed 4.7 from about 1,700 reviews.

## What we couldn't verify

- Actual prices. Capterra lists "$300 per user per month," and other third-party sites report a base fee plus per-seat pricing. None of these come from JobNimbus, and they don't agree, so we don't list a starting price.
- Vendor claims on the SumoQuote page, such as a "64% average increase in competitive close rate," "$2,078 average upgrades per signed quote," and "80% faster time to close."
- How closely today's built-in estimating matches the old standalone SumoQuote app feature for feature.
