## What it is

Square Takeoff is cloud-based construction takeoff software. You upload digital plans (PDF, TIFF, or PNG), set the scale from a known dimension or the page's preset scale, and measure areas, lengths, and counts on screen. Predefined or custom assemblies then turn those measurements into material and labor quantities, and you can export the results to Excel or PDF. The vendor also lists a roof-area tool (hips, valleys, beams, gable ends, sheathing), blueprint overlay for comparing plan revisions, a parts catalog, bid management, and team roles and permissions. Google Maps imagery can be used for site takeoffs.

It sits between quick estimate apps and full takeoff suites. If you quote residential jobs without plans, an app like [Joist](../joist/) is simpler. If you want deeper takeoff features, compare [zzTakeoff](../zztakeoff/), [STACK](../stack/), or Windows-only [PlanSwift](../planswift/). Concrete and masonry subs who want trade assemblies at a lower flat monthly price should also look at [ScopeTakeoff](../scopetakeoff/). Residential builders who want takeoff tied to quotes, ordering, and scheduling can compare [Buildxact](../buildxact/).

## Who it's for

The vendor says it's for "general contractors, subcontractors, estimators, suppliers." Its trade pages cover general contracting, concrete, drywall, plumbing, painting, framing, asphalt, insulation, landscaping, siding, flooring, roofing, masonry, electrical, and doors and windows. Capterra's vendor description pitches it to "small contractors, tradespeople and remodelers" who don't have dedicated estimating staff. Its customer quotes come from a large lumber dealer, a painting company, and a paving contractor.

## Platforms

Browser-based. The vendor says it works on "Windows, Mac or tablet" with no downloads. We didn't find a native app in the iOS App Store or Google Play listed on the vendor's site.

## What we couldn't verify

- How many users one plan covers, and what extra licenses cost.
- Whether the annual plan can be canceled or refunded partway through. One 2022 Capterra reviewer said it wasn't prorated, so read the terms before you prepay.
- Vendor claims about accuracy and time saved ("cut takeoff time from hours to minutes").
- Which "industry-standard pricing databases" it integrates with, as a third-party comparison site mentioned. The vendor's own pages don't name any.
