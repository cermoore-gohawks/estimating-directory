## What it is

FastPIPE is Windows desktop estimating software for plumbing and mechanical piping, made by FastEST, Inc. of Wichita, Kansas. The vendor says it first shipped in 1995. You load digital plans, take off fixtures, pipe, fittings, valves, and assemblies on screen, and FastPIPE adds up material and labor. The final numbers go into a Microsoft Excel bid workbook that you can customize.

The vendor lists these main features:

- **Master catalog:** over 150,000 items from manufacturers such as Tyler, Nibco, Gruvlok, Viega, Anvil, and Swagelok, with up-to-date pricing and MCAA/PHCC-based labor estimates
- **Specs:** customizable material specs and takeoff screens per job, copied from a global library
- **Auto Connect pipe:** when you take off a fitting, it looks for a line to the next fitting and adds the pipe length if the connection makes sense
- **Assemblies:** hundreds of built-in plumbing and HVAC assemblies (gas connections, boilers, pumps, VRF systems, and more) plus scalable assemblies you define
- **On-screen digitizer:** PDF, TIF, JPEG, PNG and other plan formats, plan overlay for addenda, multiple scale regions, and export of marked-up plans to PDF
- **Reports:** linear feet, diameter inches, weight, material cost, and labor hours, broken down by area, zone, cost code, and more; export to Excel, CSV, or XML

FastEST sells two sister programs on the same platform: FastDUCT for sheet metal ductwork and FastWRAP for mechanical insulation. The vendor says AI-powered takeoff is coming to all three, targeted for Q2 2027. That isn't released yet, and pricing hasn't been announced.

## Who it's for

FastEST says FastPIPE is for "commercial and industrial contractors," and that its customers range from startups with one estimator to Top 100 firms. On Capterra, 88% of reviewers are from small businesses. It fits:

- Commercial plumbing subs bidding schools, multifamily, hotels, and tenant improvements from plans
- Mechanical contractors pricing HVAC piping, medical gas, or process piping
- New plumbing company owners moving off Bluebeam counts and hand-built spreadsheets

On r/estimators, plumbing estimators recommend it for fixture counts and full piping takeoffs. One 2025 commenter warned that both the FastEST and MCAA labor rates "require a ton of tweaking" (for example, turning hanger labor down) before bids come out right.

It's not built for residential service and repair quoting. For good/better/best service quotes from a price book, see [EstimateKit](../estimatekit/) or [Joist](../joist/). For a cheaper Windows estimator aimed at residential and light commercial plumbing and electrical, compare [TurboBid](../turbobid/). If you only need counts and lengths on plans, general takeoff tools like [PlanSwift](../planswift/) or [Bluebeam Revu](../bluebeam-revu/) cost less but don't come with a piping catalog.

## Pricing (checked November 15, 2026)

FastEST publishes prices for a stand-alone install on a single PC or tablet. Network and shared-session licensing is quote-only.

**Purchase**

- First application: **$4,995 one time**
- Additional application(s): **$2,495 one time** each
- FastPIPE and FastDUCT combo: $7,490
- Includes the first year of maintenance, online training and support, installation help, material pricing updates, and a 60-day money-back guarantee

**Lease**

- First application: **$250 per month**
- Additional application(s): **$105 per month** each
- FastPIPE and FastDUCT combo: $355/mo
- Maintenance included during the lease. The vendor calls it non-binding and says you own the license after 36 payments (that's $9,000 for one application).

**Maintenance** (optional after the first year or the lease term)

- First application: $1,250 per year
- Additional application(s): $250 per year
- Covers material pricing updates, software and catalog updates, online training, and toll-free phone support

FastEST labor rates and its pricing service are included. PHCC, MCAA, and SMACNA labor rates cost extra; the price isn't published. FastPIPE also supports Ferguson, Harrison, and Allpriser pricing services.

There's no free trial or free version. You request a demo, and the 60-day money-back guarantee is the way to test it on real work. FastEST says it sells only in the US and Canada.

## Platforms

Windows only, installed on a desktop, laptop, or tablet PC. The pricing page lists:

- Windows 10 or 11, or Windows Server 2016+
- Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021, or 365
- 64-bit dual-core CPU (ARM64 needs Windows 11 emulation)
- 8 GB RAM (16 GB+ recommended), 200 MB for the app and 20 GB for job data

Teams can run it from a file server or cloud server over Remote Desktop. There's no Mac version, browser app, or mobile app.

## Ratings

- **Capterra:** 4.7/5 from 129 reviews (93 five-star, 34 four-star, 2 three-star, none lower); 4.9 for customer service and 4.8 for value. Many reviews are dated October and November 2019.
- **Software Advice and GetApp** show the same 129 reviews (same Gartner network).

Reviewers praise fast takeoff, customizable assemblies, and responsive phone support. Complaints include a dated look, confusing fitting organization in some specs, missing prices on lesser-used items, and one reviewer's report that recalculating an old bid pulls in new pricing.

## What we couldn't verify

- Whether "additional application" pricing ($2,495 or $105/mo) also covers a second FastPIPE seat, or only a second FastEST program. The page lists only a FastPIPE + FastDUCT combo as an example.
- The cost of PHCC, MCAA, and SMACNA labor rate add-ons.
- How current and complete the material pricing is for your local suppliers.
- What the AI takeoff will do or cost when it ships.
- Minimum Windows and Excel versions: the installation page lists older versions (Windows 7, Office 2013) than the pricing page does. We used the pricing page.
