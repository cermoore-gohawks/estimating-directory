## What it is

DripJobs is a CRM for painting and other home-service contractors, built around automated follow-up. When you add a lead, DripJobs tracks which pipeline stage it's in and sends pre-written texts and emails ("drips") at each step: after the estimate is booked, after the proposal goes out, before the job starts, and a review request when it's done. It ships with 40+ of these messages, and you can edit every one.

Around that it covers proposals with e-signatures, a customer portal, invoicing, ACH and card payments, customer financing, work orders, and separate calendars for estimates and jobs. The Advanced plan adds email blasts, a custom email domain, change orders, appointment reminders, good/better/best package pricing, proposal templates, Google Calendar, and Zapier.

Estimating depth comes from add-ons. **Production Rates** ($99/mo) prices work from per-unit labor and material rates for painting, pressure washing, and other trades, and **Job Costing** ($49/mo) tracks labor, materials, and overhead per job. **DripJobs Chat** ($25/mo) adds a dedicated business number with two-way texting. **Jobi AI** is an AI assistant that scores leads and drafts follow-ups; its price isn't published.

It integrates with QuickBooks (one-way, DripJobs to QuickBooks), CompanyCam for job photos, Acorn for financing, and Zapier.

It is not a takeoff tool. If you bid from drawings, measure in [STACK](../stack/) or [PlanSwift](../planswift/) first.

## Who it's for

DripJobs fits residential painting companies, and other home-service trades it lists such as roofing, flooring, remodeling, and pressure washing, that lose jobs to slow or inconsistent follow-up. It was founded by a painting contractor, which comes up often in forum discussions.

Opinions are mixed. On PaintTalk, one painter said the drip messages "have clinched me more jobs than I can count," while another who used it for a couple of months called it "clunky, glitchy" and more expensive than alternatives once everything is added up. On r/CRM, a customer of about a year said that if you quit "you lose all of the data you created" and that DripJobs wouldn't transfer the texting number. These are individual user reports, not our testing.

If your priority is fast, consistent painting estimates and polished proposals, compare [PaintScout](../paintscout/). For simple quotes and invoices across many trades at a lower price, see [Joist](../joist/).

| Jobi AI (add-on) | Contact for pricing | AI lead scoring and follow-up drafts |

DripJobs says plans are month-to-month with no contract and that you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Its FAQ states plainly that there is no free trial. The pricing page doesn't say whether annual billing is offered.

A painter running Advanced with Production Rates and Job Costing pays $295/mo; adding DripJobs Chat brings that to $320/mo, before any extra users.

## Platforms

DripJobs runs in a web browser, with free apps for iPhone and iPad (iOS 13 or later) and Android, included with every plan. The vendor says the app covers pipeline, proposals, invoicing, texting, photos, and notes, with some settings best handled on the web dashboard. It needs an internet connection; the vendor says actions sync once you're back online.

## What we couldn't verify

- The price of additional users and of Jobi AI.
- Whether annual billing or discounts are available.
- What data you can export if you cancel. One user reported losing access to their data; ask the vendor before you commit.
- Vendor claims such as "2,500+ contractors," a 4.9/5 rating, and "the #1 CRM for home service contractors." These are vendor claims, not our findings.
- The pricing page shows DripJobs Chat both as part of the Advanced plan and as a $25/mo add-on; we read it as an add-on on Pro and Advanced.
