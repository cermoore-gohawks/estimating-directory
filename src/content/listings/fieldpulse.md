## What it is

FieldPulse is field service management (FSM) software from FieldPulse (app publisher Flicent Inc), based in Dallas, Texas. It runs the office and field sides of a service business: customer records, scheduling and dispatch, work orders, estimates and invoices, payments, job costing, inventory, maintenance agreements, and a customer portal. G2's vendor profile says it is "trusted by more than 10,000 service professionals" (vendor claim).

For estimating specifically, FieldPulse says you can:

- **Build estimates on site** in the mobile app, adding labor, materials, and services, and send them by email or SMS.
- **Use preset service pricing** from a central Pricebook (services, materials, labor rates, custom categories) so quotes are consistent across techs. The Pricebook is a paid add-on.
- **Offer options:** good/better/best estimates and itemized optional add-ons that customers can pick.
- **Convert approved estimates to invoices** in one click and take payment on the spot with FieldPulse Payments.
- **Track conversion** of estimates to jobs and see which invoices are viewed, paid, or overdue.

Other things the vendor highlights: ClearPath (guided job stages for techs, included in the core platform), Maverick (an AI assistant included on all plans that can look up records and draft estimates, invoices, and jobs), and paid AI add-ons for answering calls and chat. We didn't test any of the AI features.

FieldPulse isn't a takeoff tool. It doesn't measure plans, so it isn't a fit for bidding new construction from drawings.

## Who it's for

FieldPulse's own pricing guides say it fits "established, team-based residential service businesses in the 7-to-50-technician range," with HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and garage door service as its main trades, and that "one-person operations usually have better alternatives." Capterra reports that 97% of its reviewers are small businesses.

On Reddit, FieldPulse mostly comes up as a step up from lighter tools. In an r/electricians thread from a solo electrician leaving Jobber, one reply listed it among lighter alternatives alongside Kickserv, Markate, and ServiceM8. An r/washlife thread from April 2026 asked whether anyone else felt "limited with FieldPulse after a while" as jobs grew, and a reply from an HVAC company complained that its sales rep was slow to set up booking-portal arrival windows they were paying for.

Alternatives:

- Simpler all-in-ones with published prices and free trials: [Jobber](../jobber/) or [Housecall Pro](../housecall-pro/).
- Just estimates and invoices on a phone: [Joist](../joist/).
- Good/better/best quoting with starter trade price books at a flat price: [EstimateKit](../estimatekit/).
- A ready-made flat-rate price book for HVAC, plumbing, and electrical: [Profit Rhino](../profit-rhino/).
- Bidding electrical or plumbing work from plans: [TurboBid](../turbobid/), [Electrical Bid Manager](../electrical-bid-manager/), or [FastPIPE](../fastpipe/).

## Pricing (checked November 26, 2026)

FieldPulse doesn't publish prices. From its pricing page and pricing guides:

| Item | What FieldPulse says | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essentials | Customer management, scheduling and dispatch, work orders, estimates and invoices, mobile app, custom workflows, project management, QuickBooks sync, multi-location, Open API | Not published |
| Professional | Adds deeper reporting, asset management, maintenance agreements, and the customer portal | Not published |
| Enterprise | Third package; contents not detailed on the pages we checked | Not published |
| Seats | Full-access seats (owners, office, dispatch) and lower-priced field-only seats (mobile app techs) | Not published |
| Add-ons | Pricebook, Custom Forms, Engage (VoIP and messaging), Operator AI, Chat AI, Fleet Tracking, Dynamic Proposals; "some are flat-rate, some are consumption-based, and others are seat-based" | Not published |
| Payments | Opt-in processing with its own rates; optional Next-Day Funding and Card Fee Recovery | Not published |

Notes:

- **Contract:** "You purchase a set number of seats upfront for your contract term," per the pricing FAQ. You can add seats later.
- **No free trial:** the FAQ says, "No, and we're not going to pretend otherwise." You get a live demo instead, with no credit card required.
- **Onboarding:** typically two to six weeks, per FieldPulse; QuickBooks Online setup can extend it. G2 reviewers report an average time to implement of 2 months.
- **Hidden fees:** FieldPulse says there are none and that seat rates and add-ons are laid out before you sign.
- **Third-party prices:** some comparison sites publish estimates (for example, one 2026 roundup cites a third-party-reported figure around $99/month). FieldPulse says such numbers aren't verified by it and won't match a real quote. We haven't verified any figure.

## Platforms

- **Web app** for the office.
- **iPhone and iPad app** (requires iOS 15.1 or later; version 2.16.2, updated July 2026).
- **Android app** (10K+ downloads on Google Play).
- The vendor says the mobile app works offline and can be switched to Spanish per user; the web app is English only.
- Integrations: two-way QuickBooks Online sync (customers, items, estimates, invoices, payments, purchase orders, vendors, per the vendor). G2 lists 27 integrations.

## Ratings

- **Capterra:** 4.6/5 from 534 reviews (value for money 4.4 from 501, customer service 4.6 from 504). Capterra's summary lists inconsistent payment processing, cumbersome data updates, and sync/connectivity issues as common complaints.
- **G2:** 4.7/5 from 416 reviews (85% five-star). Many recent reviews name individual FieldPulse success managers, which usually means the vendor asked customers to write them, so weigh the score accordingly.
- **Apple App Store:** 4.0/5 from 117 ratings.
- **Google Play:** 3.0/5 from about 78 reviews.

## What we couldn't verify

- Any price for any package, seat type, or add-on
- The minimum contract term and whether monthly billing is offered
- What the Enterprise package adds over Professional
- How well offline mode and the Maverick AI assistant work in practice
- The "10,000 service professionals" customer claim
