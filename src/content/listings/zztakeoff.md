## What it is

zzTakeoff is cloud-based construction takeoff software. You upload plan sets (PDF, JPG, PNG, or TIFF), scale them, and measure areas, lengths, and counts, including area cutouts. Measurements can be tied to reusable templates and assemblies so materials and quantities roll up as you go. Teams can work in the same takeoff at the same time and see each other's changes live.

zzTakeoff says its founder, Heber Allred, created PlanSwift and sold it in 2013, and that zzTakeoff is a separate product built from scratch. It isn't affiliated with PlanSwift or ConstructConnect. If you're comparing the two, see our [PlanSwift review](../planswift/).

## Who it's for

zzTakeoff markets itself to general contractors and a long list of trades: concrete, drywall, framing, masonry, landscape, roofing, insulation, electrical, plumbing, siding, flooring, painting, asphalt, and doors and windows. It's a good fit for small subs who bid from plans, want assemblies, and don't want a Windows-only desktop install or an annual contract.

It's measuring software, not an estimate-and-invoice app. If you mostly quote residential jobs without plans, an app like [Joist](../joist/) is a better match.

- The 14-day free trial needs no credit card. zzTakeoff's terms say a trial doesn't turn into a paid subscription unless you buy one.
- Paid plans renew automatically until cancelled. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period, and the terms say fees are non-refundable.
- The pricing page doesn't list team or volume discounts.

## Platforms

zzTakeoff runs in a web browser, with nothing to install. Its system recommendations list Windows 10 or newer and macOS 11 Big Sur or newer, with Chrome recommended (Edge, Safari, Firefox, and Opera also work). It suggests at least 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), a 1920 × 1080 display, a mouse, and an internet connection of 10 Mbps minimum (25 Mbps+ recommended).

There's no native mobile app. On its community forum, zzTakeoff staff suggest adding the site to the home screen on iPad, iPhone, or Android for app-like access.
