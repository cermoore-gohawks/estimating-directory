## What it is

Clear Estimates is browser-based estimating software for residential remodelers, handymen, and general contractors. It comes with a library of priced line items (parts) covering labor and materials, plus project templates. You pick a template such as a bathroom remodel or a deck, enter the dimensions, and it builds an itemized estimate using costs for your area. You can swap parts, change markups, and add your own items.

From the estimate it produces a proposal with pre-written scope language, which clients can e-sign from any device. Approved estimates convert to payable invoices, and clients can pay by credit card or ACH. Estimates export to PDF, Word, Excel, and QuickBooks, and Pro accounts can export to Buildertrend.

Clear Estimates also has an "AI Scope Assistant": you describe the project and it picks matching parts from the library to start a preliminary estimate. Pro adds what the vendor calls "AI-powered image & plan uploads." We haven't tested either AI feature.

It doesn't do on-screen plan takeoff. If you bid from drawings, measure in a takeoff tool like [STACK](../stack/), [zzTakeoff](../zztakeoff/), or [PlanSwift](../planswift/) and bring the quantities in.

## Who it's for

Clear Estimates says its parts and templates "are geared towards residential remodeling and handyman work," and that it has about 4,000 parts aimed at handymen. It fits a remodeler or handyman doing kitchens, baths, additions, painting, flooring, and similar residential jobs who wants a starting price for each line item.

The vendor is upfront that other work takes more setup. Its FAQ says commercial jobs and new home construction will need you to add parts the database doesn't have. On Reddit, remodelers describe the same limit: it's solid for standard renovation job types, but custom millwork, historic restoration, or unusual materials end up as manual entry.

If you just need quick quotes and invoices from your phone and price everything yourself, [Joist](../joist/) is cheaper.

| Standard | $79/mo | $59/mo | Unlimited AI estimates, e-signature, unlimited estimates and customers, line-item database, preloaded templates, payable invoices, quarterly labor and material rate updates, one login |
| Pro | $119/mo | $99/mo | Everything in Standard, plus AI image and plan uploads, Buildertrend integration, CRM integrations via Zapier, custom filtering and reports, property data from ATTOM, additional users at $9/mo |
| Franchise | from $249/mo | from $199/mo | Everything in Pro, plus custom branding, hosting on your website, custom feature development, and employee training. Priced by scope. |

Every new account starts with a 30-day free trial. Clear Estimates' FAQ says the first payment isn't taken until the end of the first month and you won't be charged if you cancel before then. There's no contract and no setup fee, and you can cancel from your account.

## Platforms

Clear Estimates is web-based. The vendor says it works on Windows PCs, Macs, iPads, iPhones, and Android devices in a browser with an internet connection, with no separate app to install. Its help center says it works best on a desktop.

## What we couldn't verify

- Payment processing fees for card and ACH payments. The pages we checked don't list them.
- The size of the cost library. Clear Estimates' pages variously say 12,000+, 13,000+, and 15,000+ line items, and 400+ or 500+ templates.
- Vendor claims such as "trusted by 50k+ contractors for over 20 years," the 4.7 Google and 4.5 Capterra ratings shown on its site, and that its data is "the same industry-standard data trusted by Remodeling Magazine." These are vendor claims, not our findings.
- How accurate the localized prices and AI estimates are in practice. We haven't tested them against real jobs.
