## What it is

PlanSwift is a Windows desktop takeoff and estimating application from ConstructConnect. You measure areas, lengths, and counts on digital plans with point-and-click tools, drop in material and labor assemblies, and export the bid to Excel or another estimating system. **Takeoff BOOST** is PlanSwift's suite of AI tools for automatic takeoff, counting, scaling, and bookmarking.

## Who it's for

PlanSwift lists general contractors and specialty trades: drywall, plumbing, framing, masonry, landscape, site and earthwork, electrical, concrete, HVAC, painting, and flooring. It suits estimators who already work in Windows and Excel. If you're on a Mac or want monthly billing, browser-based tools like [zzTakeoff](../zztakeoff/) (from PlanSwift's original creator) and [STACK](../stack/) are the closest alternatives.

## Pricing (verified October 26, 2026)

| Plan | Price | What's included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free trial | $0 for 14 days | Full PlanSwift Core, including all AI tools; no credit card required |
| Essential | $2,000/year, 1 seat | Takeoff and estimating, pre-built trade assemblies, Auto Count, Auto Scale, Auto Bookmark, priority support, 2 hours of live 1-on-1 training |
| Core | $3,000/year, 1 seat | Everything in Essential plus the full Takeoff BOOST suite, including **Auto Takeoff** |

- Both paid plans are annual subscriptions that include updates. PlanSwift says there are no hidden fees.
- Extra seats are discounted, and teams buying 10+ licenses may qualify for volume pricing. Those prices aren't published.
- That works out to about $167/month (Essential) or $250/month (Core), but you can't pay monthly.

## Platform requirements

- Windows 10 64-bit minimum. Windows 11 Pro or Enterprise 64-bit is recommended.
- At least 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended) and 10 GB free disk space (256 GB+ SSD recommended).
- Not supported on Mac natively, on Windows Home editions, or on server and Remote Desktop setups.
- You need an internet connection for activation and updates.
