## What it is

ConstructConnect Takeoff (often shortened to CCTO) is a web-based takeoff app from ConstructConnect, the Cincinnati company that also owns On-Screen Takeoff, PlanSwift, Quick Bid, and QuoteSoft. ConstructConnect announced the browser version as "now available" on September 30, 2026. Its help center also documents an older Windows program, now called ConstructConnect Takeoff Classic (Desktop); this review covers the browser app.

You upload plans and specs in a browser, and ConstructConnect's Takeoff BOOST tools rename, organize, and scale the sheets. Then you measure with conditions (linear, area, and count), add annotations, and export the quantities. Features the vendor highlights:

- **Auto Name and Auto Scale** (both plans): sheet names, numbers, and scale are set automatically on upload. Starter applies these to up to 25 plans per project.
- **Auto Takeoff** (Professional): AI detects and measures items and creates conditions for you. The launch post says it handles linears (walls), areas (rooms, ceilings), counts (doors, windows, plumbing fixtures), and volumes (footings, foundations, slabs).
- **Takeoff Assist** (Professional, beta): a chat assistant you can ask about the plans and specs, have it flag possible inconsistencies, and request a first-pass takeoff for supported trades.
- **Light Estimating** (beta): catalog items and assemblies that turn quantities into material and labor costs, with a vendor pricing request CSV and a client proposal export, per the release notes.
- **Collaboration:** teams work from the same cloud project. Starter allows one user only; Professional adds view-and-edit collaborators.
- **Exports:** CSV/Excel and PDF, plus exports to On-Screen Takeoff, PlanSwift, and QuoteSoft.

ConstructConnect stresses that the AI output is a starting point you have to check. Its own Auto Takeoff page says to "always review the results and ensure the final count is 100% correct for your bid."

## Who it's for

The product FAQ says it's built for "interior trade contractors: drywall, acoustical ceiling tile, painting, and flooring," and the launch post says the first release focused on interior finishes and concrete. The product page now also lists roofing, exterior elevations, landscaping, electrical, mechanical, and plumbing takeoff, but AI support for those trades looks newer, so check that your trade is supported during the trial.

On Reddit, CCTO still gets little attention. In an August 2026 r/estimators thread, an estimator whose subcontractor firm uses it said they were "surprised when almost everyone doesn't even talk about it." In a January 2026 thread, an estimator using CCTO (before the new browser app launched) said it did everything they needed in the office but ran "very clunky" on field tablets over a cellular connection.

Alternatives:

- Same vendor, Windows desktop, quote-only: [On-Screen Takeoff](../on-screen-takeoff/) (heavier commercial takeoff, pairs with Quick Bid) or [PlanSwift](../planswift/) (assemblies and Excel-style estimating).
- Cheaper browser takeoff without an annual lock-in: [zzTakeoff](../zztakeoff/), [Square Takeoff](../square-takeoff/), or [Mint Takeoff](../mint-takeoff/).
- Other AI-assisted cloud takeoff: [Togal.AI](../togal-ai/) and [STACK](../stack/).
- Commercial drywall and interiors subs who want takeoff and assembly-based estimating in one Windows program: [BuzzBID](../buzzbid/).

## Pricing (checked November 27, 2026)

From ConstructConnect's pricing page (Takeoff section):

| Plan | Price | Seats | What's included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $100/mo per seat, billed as $1,200/yr | 1 seat max, 1 user | Unlimited projects, Auto Name, Auto Scale, 25 plans per project, CSV and PDF export |
| Professional | $205/mo per seat on the plan card; $2,450/yr per seat in the comparison table; $2,200/yr first seat + $1,700/yr each additional seat in the FAQ | Unlimited paid seats | Everything in Starter plus Auto Takeoff, unlimited plans per project, view-and-edit collaborators, unlimited conditions with up to 3 quantity results |

Notes:

- **Contract:** both plans require an annual contract with annual payment. ConstructConnect says subscriptions can't be cancelled mid-term, refunded, or paused. You can upgrade any time; downgrades take effect at renewal.
- **Trial:** "Talk to sales and we'll set you up with a trial" of Professional on one of your own projects. The pricing FAQ calls it a "guided proof-of-value with training." There's no self-serve sign-up trial.
- **Takeoff Assist** is listed as Pro-only in the release notes; it's in beta.
- **Discounts:** the vendor says volume and multi-year discounts are often available through sales.
- **Conflicting Professional price:** the three figures above all appear on the same vendor page. Ask sales which applies to you and get it in writing.

## Platforms

- **Web browser** on Windows or Mac; the vendor says it runs on "Chromebook, iPad®, MAC®, PC, Android®, or iPhone®. If it opens a web page, it runs ConstructConnect Takeoff."
- No install. In Firefox and Safari, exports go straight to the Downloads folder; other browsers let you choose where to save, per the November 23, 2026 release notes.
- The takeoff view opens in a new browser tab, so pop-ups must be allowed, per the help center.

## Ratings

- **Capterra / G2:** no rating found for the browser app. (G2 has a ConstructConnect Takeoff profile but no rating was shown when we checked. The On-Screen Takeoff ratings on those sites are for the desktop product, not this one.)
- **Software Advice:** 4.5/5 from 1 review (November 2026), from a reviewer the site invited and offered a nominal incentive. Its AI summary says the reviewer felt a 1,000-page limit needed improvement.

## What we couldn't verify

- Which Professional price is current: $205/mo, $2,450/yr, or $2,200/yr first seat + $1,700 per additional seat
- How long the sales-arranged trial lasts and whether it requires a card or a signed order
- How accurate Auto Takeoff and Takeoff Assist are on real plans, or which trades they fully support today
- The vendor's claim that you can go from viewing plans to reviewing takeoff results "in under five minutes"
- Whether the 1,000-page limit mentioned in the one Software Advice review is a current product limit
- How well it performs on iPads and phones (the vendor says it runs; we didn't test it)
