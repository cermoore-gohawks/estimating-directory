## What it is

Joist is estimating, invoicing, and payment software for contractors. You build line-item estimates from saved items and templates, collect a signature on site, convert the estimate to an invoice, and take card, bank transfer, or PayPal payments. Higher tiers add work orders, change orders, reports, and job scheduling. Joist also sells an AI Receptionist that answers missed calls and captures leads. We couldn't find its price on the pricing page.

It's an estimate-and-invoice app, not a takeoff tool. If you bid from plans, measure with something like [STACK](../stack/) or [PlanSwift](../planswift/) and price the job in Joist or a spreadsheet. Electricians and plumbers who want built-in labor units can look at [TurboBid](../turbobid/).

## Who it's for

Solo and small residential contractors: handymen, remodelers, electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs, painters, landscapers, roofers, and flooring installers. Those are the trades Joist names in its own app store listings. Joist describes Basics as for "occasional jobs or businesses just starting out" and Pro as for "full-time solo contractors with a steady job flow."

## Pricing (verified October 27, 2026)

| Plan | Billed monthly | Billed annually | Key inclusions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basics | $10/mo | $8.34/mo | Up to 5 documents/month, online payments, estimates to invoices, AI tools, homeowner financing |
| Pro | $17/mo | $14.17/mo | Unlimited documents and clients, logo, line items and photos, client activity tracking, work orders |
| Elite | $32/mo | $26.67/mo | License and insurance on documents, change orders, business and sales reports, Joist Reviews |
| Run | $70/mo intro (listed at $100/mo) | $58.33/mo | Everything in Elite plus jobs, scheduling visits, and linking jobs to estimates and invoices |

The 14-day free trial gives full access to Run features, with no credit card required. Joist says there are no setup fees. Standard payment processing fees apply when you accept payments through Joist Payments. Joist's FAQ says the free plan is no longer available.

**Unverified:** The Basics card on the pricing page also shows a $12 figure next to the $10 monthly price. It may be a crossed-out list price, but we couldn't confirm that. Run's $70 price is labeled an introductory 30% discount, and the page doesn't say when it ends. The plan comparison table didn't show which tier includes QuickBooks sync, so check with Joist if you need it.

## Platforms

Joist has native apps for iPhone and iPad (App Store) and Android (Google Play), plus a web app. It's cloud-based, so data syncs across devices. The App Store listing says payments currently work only in the US and Canada.
