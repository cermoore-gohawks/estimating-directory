## What it is

RoofSnap is roofing and gutter software from a Denver-based company that combines roof measurement with estimating and getting paid. You can order a measurement report from RoofSnap's team, or draw the roof yourself over aerial imagery with the in-app sketch tool: you trace the roof lines, enter pitches, and RoofSnap calculates areas, linear measurements, and waste. The vendor says DIY measurements work from aerial imagery, photos, or drone footage.

Measurements flow into the estimating side. Instant Estimates auto-fills roof area and pitch from a property address and gives you a link to share with the homeowner. Good-better-best options and a detailed estimate builder with your own materials, markups, overhead, and commission rates are also included. From there you can send material orders, e-sign contracts, and take card or ACH payments through RoofSnap Payments (powered by Stripe). Homeowner financing is offered through Acorn Finance.

If you'd rather have a roofing CRM with job boards and automations, compare [Roofr](../roofr/). Contractors bidding commercial roofs from blueprints should look at a plan takeoff tool such as [PlanSwift](../planswift/) or [STACK](../stack/).

## Who it's for

Solo roofers and small residential roofing crews, plus gutter and holiday-lighting installers who need eave and rake lengths. RoofSnap's homepage says it's "built for small crews and solo roofers." It suits contractors who measure often enough to want unlimited DIY sketching, and occasional users who just want to order a report now and then.

From RoofSnap's pricing page, in USD:

| Option | Listed price | What's included (per vendor) |

| Pay-As-You-Go | Starting at $13 per measurement order | Reports ordered from RoofSnap, about 4-hour delivery, rush in as little as 30 minutes, no subscription |

- The page doesn't list an annual price for a single user.
- Payment processing is listed at "as low as" 2.9% + $0.30 per card transaction and 1% per ACH charge.
- The page also advertises "Get your first order free."

## Platforms

Browser-based with cloud project storage, plus RoofSnap apps on the iPhone App Store and Google Play. When we checked, the apps were rated about 3.3/5 (40 ratings) on iOS and 3.7/5 on Google Play, well below the 4.7/5 on Capterra. Recent app reviews mention bugs and slow updates, so test the app on your own phone or tablet during the trial.

## What we couldn't verify

- Measurement accuracy. We haven't compared RoofSnap reports or DIY sketches against hand measurements. App Store reviews include both praise and complaints about accuracy.
- Which pay-as-you-go report costs $13, since the report table starts at $35 for non-subscribers.
- The homepage's "Rated 4.5/5 stars" claim, which doesn't say where the rating comes from.
- Vendor claims about rush delivery times and insurance acceptance of reports.
