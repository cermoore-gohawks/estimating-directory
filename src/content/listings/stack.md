## What it is

STACK is a cloud-based construction takeoff and estimating platform from STACK Construction Technologies (Cincinnati, OH). You upload plans, measure them in the browser, and on the Pro tier turn those quantities into a priced estimate and proposal. STACK also sells a separate field-management product, **Build & Operate**.

## Who it's for

STACK markets to specialty subcontractors, general contractors, suppliers, and owners. The vendor's FAQ lists concrete, drywall, flooring, insulation, landscaping, masonry, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, painting, roofing, and structural steel. Because it's browser-based, it's one of the few full takeoff tools that works on a Mac without Windows.

## Pricing (verified October 26, 2026)

| Plan | Price | Key limits / features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 7 days of Pro first, then 1 active project/month, up to 300 plan pages, 50 measurements/project, core takeoff tools |
| Premium | $299 per user/month, billed annually | Unlimited projects, pages, and measurements; full takeoff toolset; item and assembly library; drawing overlay; AI takeoffs; unlimited viewer seats |
| Pro | **Not verified** (price wasn't in the page text we could read) | Everything in Premium plus worksheet-style estimating, STACK for Excel, regional BNI cost library, proposal generation; ERP connectors as an add-on |

- All Takeoff & Estimate subscriptions are **12-month terms**. You can pay upfront (STACK says this saves 20%) or spread the payments monthly.
- STACK's main pricing page also lists **$49/user/month** for Build & Operate (plan viewing and markup, punch lists, daily reports) and **$348/user/month** for a combined takeoff + estimating + field package. Build & Operate offers a two-week free trial.
- STACK advertises discounts for military and veterans, recent graduates, minority-owned businesses, and students. We didn't verify the discount amounts.

## Key features

- Measurement types: linear (and linear with drop for electrical and HVAC), area with cut-outs, pitched roof calculations, volume for concrete, arcs, and counts.
- AI tools: automatic plan naming and hyperlinking, symbol search and recognition, and AI takeoffs (Premium and up). AI aerial image takeoffs are a paid add-on.
- Drawing version overlay to catch changes between plan revisions.
- Integrations through STACK's partner network, plus a mobile field app for iOS, Android, and Windows (via the app stores linked from STACK's site).
