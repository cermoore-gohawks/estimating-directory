## What it is

Roofr is roofing-specific software that combines satellite **roof measurement reports**, an **Instant Estimator** lead tool for homeowners, proposals with e-signatures, material ordering, invoicing and payments, and a roofing CRM with job boards.

## Who it's for

Residential roofing contractors, from owner-operators to growing sales teams. Roofr's own guidance: Essentials suits new or 1–2 person teams, and Scale suits teams of 3+ running multiple jobs.

## Pricing (verified October 26, 2026)

| Plan | Monthly billing | Key inclusions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $0/mo | $19 measurement reports (24-hour delivery), 1 basic job board, 10 trial proposals/invoices/work orders, 5 automated actions, unlimited users |
| Measure+ | $109/mo (Roofr's comparison table shows $109–$169 depending on delivery speed) | $13 reports, guaranteed 2- or 6-hour delivery, material calculations, waste factor, CSV downloads, branding |
| Essentials | $249/mo | $13 reports in 2 hours or less, unlimited proposals/invoices/work orders, card and ACH payments, e-signatures |
| Scale | $349/mo ($299/mo billed yearly) | 7 job boards, crew management, 25+ automations, reporting, job costing, QuickBooks, role permissions |

**Add-ons:** Instant Estimator ($149/mo, or $125/mo billed yearly), Roofr Sites ($99/mo, or $85/mo yearly), AI Receptionist ($99/mo, or $85/mo yearly). ESX files cost $12 per report on Starter and $10 on Measure+.

**Unverified or inconsistent:** Roofr's pricing page lists "$209/mo billed yearly" for both Measure+ and Essentials. That's more than Measure+'s $109 monthly price, so it can't be right for both plans. Roofr's FAQ says annual billing saves about 15%. Confirm annual pricing with Roofr directly.

## Platforms

Roofr runs in a web browser. On phones, Roofr says it's deliberately *not* an App Store or Google Play app. You add the mobile web app to your iPhone or Android home screen, and it supports push notifications. You need an internet connection for most actions.

## What we couldn't verify
