## What it is

ScopeTakeoff is web-based takeoff and estimating software aimed at specialty subcontractors. You upload PDF plans, measure quantities in the browser, drop those quantities into trade-specific assemblies (material, labor, equipment, waste), and export a Schedule of Values for GC submission or a client-facing proposal.

The product is strongest in **concrete**: the vendor's concrete page lists assemblies for slab on grade (4″/5″/6″), continuous and spread footings, grade beams, cast-in-place and retaining walls, columns, rebar by bar size (#3–#8) with lap splices and dowels, formwork priced per SFCA (including reuse factors and ICF), curb and gutter, paving, and repair/overlay scopes. Measured SF and depth feed automatic cubic-yard calculations. A parallel **masonry** library covers CMU (4″–12″), brick and stone veneer, grout/mortar volumes, bond beams, lintels, and restoration/tuckpointing.

The founder, Keaton Bandhoesingh (Marietta, Georgia), also runs Foundation Construction Group, a concrete and masonry subcontracting company. ScopeTakeoff markets itself as built by a sub for subs, not as a GC project-management suite.

It is not an estimate-and-invoice phone app. If you mostly quote residential service work without plans, compare [Joist](../joist/). For Windows desktop takeoff with long-standing trade plugins, see [PlanSwift](../planswift/). For a broader cloud takeoff platform with a free tier, see [STACK](../stack/). For lower-cost browser takeoff without ScopeTakeoff's pre-built trade SOV workflow, see [zzTakeoff](../zztakeoff/).

## Who it's for

Concrete and masonry subcontractors bidding commercial or residential work from plan sets, plus other specialty trades the vendor lists in the same subscription: drywall, painting, HVAC, flooring, landscaping, roofing, remodeling, asphalt, electrical, plumbing, tile, siding, fence, and windows & doors. The FAQ describes Solo (1 seat) and Team (2–5 seats with bid review and multi-entity profiles for multi-state licenses).

A poor fit if you need offline desktop software, deep Excel database import, field CRM/scheduling/payments, or a tool with a large independent user community already documented on contractor forums.

- No annual contract, no setup/implementation fee, no per-trade add-ons. Cancel from account settings; access continues through the current billing period; estimates are exportable.
- Currency is shown as bare "$100" on USD-facing pages; confirm at checkout if you are outside the US.
- Competitor prices in ScopeTakeoff marketing tables (STACK, PlanSwift, Procore, Buildertrend) are **vendor claims**. Cross-check our [STACK](../stack/) and [PlanSwift](../planswift/) reviews for figures we verified separately.

## Platforms

Fully web-based in a modern browser on Windows, Mac, tablet, or phone. The FAQ says there is no desktop installer and estimates sync in the cloud. We did not find native App Store / Google Play apps.

Signup is self-serve at [app.scopetakeoff.com/signup](https://app.scopetakeoff.com/signup).

## What we couldn't verify

- Hands-on accuracy of cubic-yard, rebar, forming, and CMU assembly math against a stamped set of drawings.
- Whether every trade named on the about/FAQ pages (16+) has the same assembly depth as concrete and masonry.
- How many active paying customers exist beyond the Capterra listing (9 reviews, overall 5.0, when checked November 6, 2026) and the testimonials on the vendor site.
- Exact STACK / PlanSwift / Procore prices quoted in ScopeTakeoff comparison tables (those figures disagree with other published pages we have reviewed).
- Support response times and whether chat listed on Capterra is always staffed.
