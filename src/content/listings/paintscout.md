## What it is

PaintScout is estimating and sales software built only for painting contractors. You set up your production rates (how fast your crew paints a given surface) and material costs once, then build an estimate on site by entering areas, surfaces, prep, and coats. It turns that into a branded, interactive proposal the customer can view, chat about, sign, and pay a deposit on.

The core **Sales** plan covers estimates, proposals, e-signatures, payments, automated email and SMS follow-ups, reminders, a drag-and-drop sales pipeline, task tracking, two-way Google Calendar sync, and close-rate reporting. The **Operations** add-on adds customer and job management and job scheduling, so a sold job flows into production.

It integrates with QuickBooks Online and Xero for accounting, CompanyCam for job photos, Stripe for payments, Wisetack for customer financing, and Zapier for anything else.

It is not a takeoff tool: it doesn't measure from plans. If you bid painting from drawings, measure in a takeoff tool like [STACK](../stack/) or [PlanSwift](../planswift/) first. If you just need simple quotes and invoices across many trades, [Joist](../joist/) is cheaper; for AI-drafted remodel estimates, see [Handoff](../handoff/).

## Who it's for

PaintScout is for painting companies, from owner-operators to firms with several estimators, that want consistent pricing and professional proposals. On r/paint, a painter who switched from Joist said PaintScout "cuts the time to quote down significantly," and another said it was "head and shoulders above" five similar products for ease of use and presentation, though templates "take a while" to set up.

Painters who mainly want automated marketing follow-ups and an all-in-one CRM often compare it with DripJobs, a painting CRM. We haven't reviewed DripJobs yet.

Every account includes free onboarding and live chat support. Optional one-time Success Packages cost $999 (Plus), $1,499 (Pro, adds two training sessions), or $1,999 (Premium, adds three training sessions and three presentation build-outs); all include production-rate setup.

The pricing page doesn't say how many users the base plan includes, and it doesn't advertise a free trial. Getting started is by booking a demo.

## Platforms

PaintScout runs in a web browser (Safari, Chrome, or Firefox) on computers, tablets, and phones; Apple devices need iOS 11 or later. You can add it to your phone's home screen for app-like access. Its offline mode stores estimates in the browser and syncs them when you reconnect, but you can't send, search, or download while offline.
