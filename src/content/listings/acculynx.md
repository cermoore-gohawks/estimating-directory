## What it is

AccuLynx (made by ExactLogix, Inc., based in Wisconsin) is **all-in-one business management software for roofing contractors**: CRM and lead tracking, estimating and proposals, production scheduling, material ordering, invoicing, payments, and reporting, in a web app with iOS and Android field apps. The vendor calls it the "#1 roofing software" and says it is "designed for roofing companies that primarily focus on residential insurance restoration and retail roofing."

For estimating, the relevant pieces are:

- **Measurement-driven estimates:** order EagleView, GAF QuickMeasure, or Geospan reports from inside AccuLynx, or sync Hover, RoofSnap, and RoofScope measurements. Measurements fill in the estimate automatically instead of being typed in. (Hover and RoofSnap need the paid AppConnections add-on.)
- **Live supplier pricing:** pull your real-time account pricing from ABC Supply, QXO, or SRS Distribution into each estimate, then place the material order directly from the job.
- **Templates and margins:** build estimates from templates or from scratch, adjust waste factors per line item, add labor and other trades, and set your profit margin with a slider. The vendor says it uses custom calculations from the full measurement data rather than only pricing per square.
- **Mobile estimating:** the AccuLynx Field App can build estimates on site, calculate material, labor, and margin, and present proposals with financing options. Creating proposals and collecting signatures in the app requires the **Smart(er) Docs** add-on.
- **Payments and financing:** card, debit, and ACH processing plus consumer financing options.

It is not a plan-takeoff tool. There's no measuring from blueprints or PDFs; roof quantities come from aerial or app-based measurement reports.

## Who it's for

Residential roofing companies with a sales team and at least a few office or field users. AccuLynx's FAQ says it serves "growing startups with as little as 3 users" up to "multi-location operations with hundreds of users." Its Elite plan adds tools aimed at insurance work (supplement tracking, mortgage-check tracking) and at larger operations (multiple locations, production calendar, commissions).

On r/Roofing, AccuLynx comes up in nearly every "which CRM should I use" thread, usually against JobNimbus. In a January 2026 thread from a roofer starting a company, replies described AccuLynx as "good but pricey and rigid" and said it "largely operates within their prescribed workflows with limited flexibility," while JobNimbus was called more customizable but more work to set up. Other replies said it's a good pick for hail and insurance work because supplier ordering and the insurance workflow are built in, but may be more than a startup needs. A September 2025 commenter using JobNimbus said they preferred AccuLynx "from the field standpoint." (We ignored replies in these threads that promoted the poster's own products.)

Alternatives:

- For a roofing CRM with built-in SumoQuote estimating, a free trial, and more flexible workflows, see [JobNimbus](../jobnimbus/).
- For cheaper roof measurement reports plus proposals with published prices, see [Roofr](../roofr/).
- For DIY roof sketching and measurement reports for small crews, see [RoofSnap](../roofsnap/) (which also syncs into AccuLynx).

## Pricing (checked November 19, 2026)

AccuLynx publishes **no prices**. From its own pages:

| Plan | Price | Notable features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | Custom quote | CRM, AI lead scoring, estimates and contracts, custom materials library, aerial measurement ordering, supplier direct ordering, invoicing, labor management and work orders, mobile field app, job costing, profit forecasting, P&L |
| Elite | Custom quote | Everything in Pro plus custom workflow manager, multiple locations, embedded web forms, supplement, permit, and mortgage-check tracking, production calendar and status reports, sales pipeline and prospecting reports, commissions, advances and paybacks |

Notes:

- **Billing:** a monthly subscription fee for each user license, based on users and features, per the homepage FAQ.
- **Add-ons (unpriced):** Customer Portal, Smart(er) Docs, AccuFi, ReportsPlus, DataMart, Texting, and Mobile Crew App are listed on the plan page; AppConnections is required for some integrations, such as Hover and RoofSnap.
- **Contract:** "No contracts... you can cancel anytime," per the vendor.
- **Free trial:** none advertised. AccuLynx offers a 30-minute demo and a self-guided tour. Capterra lists the free trial as not available.
- **Onboarding:** the vendor says training and live phone support are included and most customers are up and running "in as little as a week."
- **Third-party figures (not confirmed by AccuLynx):** Roofing Software Guide and EaveWorks (which sells competing software) report about **$60 to $120 per user per month** and say a **$250/month "Essential"** plan was dropped in August 2026. Some third parties also report onboarding fees; AccuLynx doesn't list one. Treat all of these as rough guides and get a written quote with every add-on priced.

## Platforms

- **Web app** in the browser (production calendar and detailed company reports are web-only, per the vendor)
- **AccuLynx Field** app for iPhone, iPad, and Android (estimates, photos, call logging, proposals)
- **AccuLynx Crew** app for in-house crews and subcontractors, with Spanish support (Mobile Crew App is listed as an add-on)

## Ratings

- **Capterra:** 4.6/5 from 848 reviews, with 95% positive sentiment in Capterra's summary. Pros flagged: an intuitive interface and centralized workflow. Cons flagged: "high and escalating costs" and cumbersome invoicing. A May 2026 reviewer wrote that AccuLynx "has repeatedly raised the price" and that add-ons "can really shoot up your bill" for a smaller company.
- **Apple App Store (AccuLynx Field):** 3.8/5 from 599 ratings
- **Google Play (AccuLynx Field):** 4.1/5 from 133 reviews, 10K+ downloads, updated October 7, 2026
- **G2:** AccuLynx's estimating page says "91% of users love AccuLynx's estimating features" per a G2 Grid report. That's a vendor claim; we didn't check G2 directly.

## Ownership note

In July 2025 Verisk, the company behind the Xactimate insurance estimating software, agreed to buy AccuLynx for $2.35 billion. Verisk tried to terminate the deal in December 2025 after the FTC's review ran past the deadline. The Delaware Court of Chancery ruled the termination invalid in August 2026, and the Delaware Supreme Court affirmed on October 23, 2026. Verisk must keep pursuing FTC clearance, and says "there is no guarantee that the transaction will be completed." Some roofing contractors have objected to Xactimate's owner holding their business data. If you sign up, watch for changes to pricing, integrations, or data terms if the deal closes.
