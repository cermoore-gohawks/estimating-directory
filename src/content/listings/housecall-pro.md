## What it is

Housecall Pro (made by Codefied Inc.) is **field service management software** for home service businesses. It covers online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates, invoicing, payments, and reviews in one web app with iOS and Android apps. The vendor says it is used by more than 200,000 pros.

For estimating, the relevant pieces are:

- **Estimates:** quotes built from templates or your price book, sent by text or email, with customer e-signature, multiple options, and change orders. The vendor says you can draft estimates "in seconds with AI." When a customer approves an option, you can copy it to a job.
- **Price book:** your own services and materials with images and descriptions. Housecall Pro says it can show how your pricing compares with similar businesses "based on millions of real jobs," which we couldn't verify.
- **Flat-rate pricing** (Essentials and up): builds profit margins into service prices.
- **Sales proposal tool:** visual good/better/best proposals with side-by-side comparisons and financing offers. $40/month on Basic and Essentials, included on MAX.
- **Profit Rhino price book:** a pre-built flat-rate book for HVAC, plumbing, drain cleaning, and electrical, hosted inside Housecall Pro. See our [Profit Rhino review](../profit-rhino/) for the standalone product.

It does **not** do plan takeoff: there's no way to measure blueprints or PDFs, and no assemblies or labor-unit database for commercial bids.

## Who it's for

Solo operators and small crews in residential service and repair: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, handyman, garage door, cleaning, and similar trades. It fits shops that quote at the customer's door or from a phone call and want the quote to flow into the job and invoice.

Contractors ask about it often. In an r/Construction thread ("Is housecall worth it"), a new user on Essentials said pricing "didn't seem too crazy" until the demo showed many add-ons, and that he was paying $50/month extra for a pipeline feature. A reply from a six-month user liked it for scheduling and invoicing but said some tasks can't be done on mobile and the set-pricing option wasn't yet worth paying extra for. On r/business, a Canadian generator service company said it switched 10 months earlier and was happy with service plans and the price book. (We ignored replies in those threads that were promoting competing apps.)

Alternatives:

- For the closest all-in-one competitor, with a $29/month (annual) entry plan and Home Depot pricing built into quotes, see [Jobber](../jobber/).
- For a simpler, cheaper estimate-and-invoice app, see [Joist](../joist/).
- For good/better/best quoting with starter trade price books, see [EstimateKit](../estimatekit/).
- For a standalone flat-rate book you can plug into other software, see [Profit Rhino](../profit-rhino/).
- If you bid plumbing, mechanical, or electrical work from plans, look at [FastPIPE](../fastpipe/), [TurboBid](../turbobid/), or [Electrical Bid Manager](../electrical-bid-manager/).

## Pricing (checked November 18, 2026)

From Housecall Pro's pricing page:

| Plan | Monthly billing | Billed annually | Users included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $79/mo | $59/mo | 1 |
| Essentials | $189/mo | $149/mo | 5 |
| MAX | $329/mo | $299/mo | 8 (+$35/mo per additional user) |

Notes:

- The page was showing a **"50% off for 3 months"** promotion on monthly billing ($39.50, $94.50, and $164.50 for the first three months). Treat it as temporary.
- **Basic** includes online booking, scheduling and dispatch, estimates and invoicing, payments and consumer financing, price book, job costing, and review management.
- **Essentials** adds routes, flat-rate pricing, checklists, photo reports, employee GPS tracking, commissions, and QuickBooks Online sync.
- **MAX** adds the sales proposal tool, recurring service plans, route optimization, open API and Zapier, a dedicated onboarding specialist, and escalated phone support.
- **Add-ons:** sales proposals $40/month (below MAX), per the estimating feature page. Profit Rhino price book $199/month for one or all trade books, per Housecall Pro's help center; some third-party guides list it at $149/month. Other add-ons (CSR AI, Voice, Pipeline, Payroll, vehicle GPS, websites) are listed on the pricing page without prices.
- **Payments:** card processing "as low as 2.59%"; bank payments 1%.
- **Free trial:** 14 days of the MAX plan, no credit card required. The vendor says your data is kept if the trial lapses.
- **Contract:** the FAQ says no long-term contract, and you can upgrade anytime or downgrade at the end of the billing period.
- Prices are in USD and exclude sales tax.

## Ratings

- **Capterra:** 4.7/5 from about 2,750 reviews (ease of use 4.6, customer service 4.7). Recent critical reviews mention pop-ups interrupting web workflows and a desktop layout that's weaker than the mobile app.
- **Apple App Store:** 4.7/5 from about 32,000 ratings
- **Google Play:** 4.5/5 from about 6,500 to 7,000 reviews (the count differed between page views), 500K+ downloads, updated November 9, 2026
- **G2 and Trustpilot:** G2 and Trustpilot block automated access, so we couldn't check them directly. A third-party tracker (TradeAdWatch) reported G2 4.3/5 from 211 reviews and Trustpilot 2.9/5 from 702 reviews on November 4, 2026, with Trustpilot complaints centered on billing, support, and sudden software changes.

## What we couldn't verify

- Whether Basic or Essentials can add users beyond the included count, and at what price
- The current Profit Rhino add-on price ($199/month in the help center vs $149/month in third-party guides)
- Prices for CSR AI, Voice, Pipeline, Payroll, and other add-ons
- How long the 50%-off promotion runs
- Vendor claims of 35% average revenue growth and 8+ hours saved per week
- How the "AI" estimate drafting works and which plans include it
