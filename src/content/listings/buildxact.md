## What it is

Buildxact is cloud-based estimating and job-management software for residential construction. You can measure on uploaded plans (digital takeoff), build estimates from your own rates, recipes, and live supplier pricing, then send a branded quote for digital signature. Higher plans add scheduling built from the estimate, job management, client selections, a client portal, purchase and work orders, timesheets, invoicing, and accounting integration (the vendor lists QuickBooks Online, Xero, and MYOB).

The vendor's AI assistant, Blu, is spread across the plans: an Assembly Assistant (Foundation and up), an Estimate Generator that builds an estimate from a description (Go and Pro and up), a Takeoff Assistant that the vendor says auto-scales and auto-measures plans, and an Estimate Reviewer that flags missed items (both included in Master or as Pro add-ons).

It's a bigger tool than a pure takeoff app. If you only need to measure plans, compare [zzTakeoff](../zztakeoff/), [ScopeTakeoff](../scopetakeoff/), [Square Takeoff](../square-takeoff/), or [STACK](../stack/). If you quote small residential jobs without plans, [Joist](../joist/) or [Clear Estimates](../clear-estimates/) are lighter and cheaper.

## Who it's for

The vendor pitches it to "residential builders, remodelers and trades" and says it's used by over 7,000 construction companies. Its customer stories are mostly remodelers, kitchen-and-bath shops, and small home builders. The Foundation plan is aimed at "builders, general contractors and trades who want to win more jobs," Pro at contractors managing multiple projects and teams, and Master at "established builders."

## Pricing (verified November 12, 2026)

From Buildxact's US pricing page, in USD. Annual plans are 15% cheaper and require a 12-month commitment.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Billed annually | Main features (per vendor) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Go | $0 | $0 | 5 credits for AI estimates and proposals, Home Depot ordering, pay as you go |
| Foundation | $199/mo | $2,030/yr | Unlimited users, lead management, digital takeoffs, Blu Assembly Assistant, quote letters, digital signatures, dealer integration, purchase orders, training and support |
| Pro | $399/mo | $4,070/yr | Foundation plus Blu Estimate Generator, job management, schedules, Onsite mobile app |
| Master | $599/mo | $6,110/yr | Pro plus Blu Takeoff Assistant, Blu Estimate Reviewer, user access controls, priority support |

- Pro add-ons: Blu Takeoff Assistant +$99/mo, Blu Estimate Reviewer +$149/mo.
- The help center says the 14-day free trial needs no credit card and expires on its own.

## Platforms

Web browser. The Pro and Master plans include the Buildxact Onsite mobile app for crews in the field. A third-party review describes the mobile app as a stripped-down version without offline mode; we didn't test it.

## What we couldn't verify

- What a Go credit costs to top up, and how many credits one AI estimate or proposal uses.
- Whether monthly billing also requires a 12-month term. A third-party review says it does; the vendor's pricing page only says this for annual plans.
- Vendor performance claims such as "75% less estimating time" and "2x+ sales growth."
- How accurate the Blu Takeoff Assistant's auto-measuring is on real plans.
